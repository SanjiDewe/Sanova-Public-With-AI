Privacy

How Sanova handles information used to provide your account, workspace, AI features, and connected services.

1. Information Sanova Handles

Sanova may handle information needed to create and secure your account, operate your workspace, provide requested features, and maintain service reliability. This can include account identifiers, authentication/session information, preferences, settings, activity needed to operate requested features, and technical information such as logs.

2. AI Conversations and Requests

When you use Sanova AI, your prompts, conversation state, tool requests, and related results may be processed to provide the requested AI functionality. AI-related data is handled according to the configuration of the Sanova service and the provider selected for the request.

3. AI Providers and Models

Sanova is designed so that the AI provider and model can be changed through supported configuration and user settings. When an external AI provider is selected, information required to process the request may be sent to that provider. The provider's own privacy terms and data-handling practices may therefore apply to that processing.

Sanova does not require a single AI provider to be hardcoded into the application. Availability of providers and models may change over time.

4. Tools, Integrations, and Connected Accounts

Some Sanova features can interact with external services or connected accounts. Sanova only uses those connections to perform features you request and that your account is authorized to perform. Data received from a connected service may be processed to complete the requested operation, display results, maintain necessary state, or record security and audit information.

5. AI Actions and Approval

Sanova AI may prepare an action proposal using available tools. Where an action requires user approval, Sanova is designed to require an explicit approval before the action is handed to the execution system. Approval, rejection, execution status, and related audit information may be retained as necessary for security, troubleshooting, and accountability.

6. Security and Audit Information

Sanova may maintain authentication records, security events, execution records, and audit information to protect accounts, investigate failures or misuse, and maintain the integrity of requested operations. Security records are not intended to be used as a substitute for the user's own records.

7. Cookies and Sessions

Sanova may use cookies or similar session mechanisms required to authenticate users, maintain sessions, preserve security state, and provide requested functionality.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, satisfy operational requirements, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable obligations. Specific retention periods may differ by data type and feature.

9. Your Choices

You can manage supported account, personalization, AI provider/model, and connected-service settings through Sanova. Depending on the feature and applicable law, you may also have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information.

10. Third-Party Services

When Sanova connects to an external provider or service, that service may process information under its own terms and privacy policy. Sanova cannot control independent processing performed by third parties outside Sanova's systems.

11. Changes

This Privacy page may be updated when Sanova's features, integrations, security practices, or legal requirements change. The current version published by Sanova is the version that applies to the service at that time.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official contact method provided by the Sanova service.
